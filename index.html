ⓘ limitations & details

ⓘ how to read this

Chi-square attack over sequential LSB chunks. Sustained p > 0.5 across most chunks is consistent with LSB-embedded data; red blocks mark locally suspect areas on the LSB plane.

Compares the file’s quantization tables against the standard IJG curve to estimate encoder quality and flag anomalies such as diverging luma/chroma qualities (double-compression hint). JPEG only.

ⓘ how to read this
